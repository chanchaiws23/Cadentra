# Cadentra — English store listing

## App name

Cadentra

## Short description

Plan tasks, build habits, and focus consistently at your own pace.

## Full description

Cadentra brings your schedule, tasks, goals, habits, and focus sessions together so you can see what matters today and restart gently when plans change.

- Plan days and weeks with a calendar and time blocking
- Organize tasks, recurring work, milestones, and long-term goals
- Track different habit types without punishing a missed day
- Use a focus timer and record interruptions and actual time
- Reflect daily and review progress each week
- Use AI Coach through propose, preview, and confirm before anything is saved
- Optionally connect Google Calendar and Health Connect
- View recent data and queue temporary changes while offline

Row Level Security isolates each account's personal data. Cadentra uploads only daily totals for steps, sleep, and exercise from Health Connect. Connections, exports, and account deletion remain under your control in Settings.

Cadentra has no ads or social competition. It is designed to help you build consistency with a calmer, kinder system.
